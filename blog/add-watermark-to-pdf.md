# How to Add a Watermark to a PDF Online

A watermark is a faint text or image overlay placed across the pages of a document. You've probably seen watermarks like "CONFIDENTIAL," "DRAFT," or a company logo running diagonally across a page. Adding a watermark to your PDF is a simple way to protect your work, brand your documents, or indicate the status of a file — and it can be done online in just a few steps.

## Why Add a Watermark to a PDF?

- **Protect intellectual property**: Photographers, designers, and writers often watermark sample work with their name or logo to discourage unauthorized use before a final purchase.
- **Mark document status**: Labels like "DRAFT," "SAMPLE," or "CONFIDENTIAL" help readers immediately understand the nature of the document.
- **Branding**: Businesses add their logo or company name as a subtle watermark on official documents, reports, and proposals.
- **Tracking and authenticity**: Some organizations add unique identifiers to watermarks to track which copy of a document was shared with which recipient.

## How to Add a Watermark to a PDF Online

1. **Open the watermark tool** in your browser.
2. **Upload your PDF file** from your device or cloud storage.
3. **Choose the watermark type** — text (like "CONFIDENTIAL" or your name) or an image (such as a logo).
4. **Customize the appearance** — adjust the font, size, color, opacity, rotation angle, and position of the watermark.
5. **Select which pages** to apply the watermark to — all pages, or specific ones.
6. **Apply and download** your watermarked PDF.

## Choosing Watermark Settings

- **Opacity**: A lower opacity (around 20-40%) keeps the watermark visible without obscuring the underlying text — ideal for documents that still need to be read clearly.
- **Position**: Diagonal watermarks across the center of the page are common because they're harder to crop out, while corner watermarks (like a logo) are more subtle.
- **Color**: Light gray or muted colors work well for text watermarks so they don't distract from the main content.
- **Size**: Make sure the watermark text or logo is large enough to be visible but not so large that it overwhelms the page.

## Text Watermarks vs. Image Watermarks

- **Text watermarks** are quick to set up and work well for status labels like "DRAFT," "COPY," or "CONFIDENTIAL," as well as for adding a name or date across pages.
- **Image watermarks** are better for branding purposes, such as placing a company logo on every page of a proposal, report, or invoice template.

Many tools allow you to save your watermark settings, so you can quickly reapply the same logo or text to future documents without reconfiguring everything each time.

## Common Use Cases

- Freelancers watermarking draft designs or photos sent to clients before final payment.
- Businesses adding "Confidential" labels to internal reports shared outside the company.
- Students or researchers marking early drafts of papers to distinguish them from final submissions.
- Real estate agents adding their branding to property brochures and floor plans.

## Is It Safe to Add Watermarks Online?

Online watermarking tools process your PDF temporarily to apply the overlay and generate the new file for download. As always, use tools with secure connections, and avoid uploading highly sensitive files to unfamiliar websites if privacy is a major concern.

## Final Thoughts

Adding a watermark to your PDF is a quick way to protect your work, communicate a document's status, or reinforce your branding. With a free online tool, you can customize text or image watermarks and apply them to your documents in just a couple of minutes — no design software required.
